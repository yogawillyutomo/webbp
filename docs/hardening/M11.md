## Objective

Use the M10 content architecture to create individually addressable public product pages without introducing new unsupported claims or a CMS dependency.

The tranche is intentionally narrow. Existing normalized product records already contain public maturity, status detail, description, technology, evidence-safe proof, and solution relationships. M11 makes that content shareable and indexable instead of inventing a new editorial domain.

## Implemented scope

- `/products/[slug]` static route generated from published canonical product records;
- five published product detail pages generated as static HTML;
- product metadata and canonical URL derived from the normalized content repository;
- public status, description, evidence, technology, and related-solution presentation;
- portfolio modal CTA to the canonical product route;
- nested-route Navbar/Footer links back to homepage sections;
- sitemap inclusion for every published product;
- smoke gate discovers product routes from the sitemap and verifies every exposed product detail page;
- unknown product slug is verified against the established branded 404 behavior.

## Evidence/claim guardrails

This tranche does not:

- upgrade product maturity;
- invent customer deployments or outcomes;
- claim production adoption, revenue, SLA, benchmark, or accuracy not already approved in canonical public content;
- create case studies without supporting evidence;
- introduce a blog/newsroom merely to increase page count;
- adopt a headless CMS;
- move operational product behavior into WEBBP.
